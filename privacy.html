The H Stock Management Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 (accounts and activity log added)

The H Stock Management is a tool for counting stock with a team. This page explains what the app stores and why.

What we store

  • Stock data: product barcodes, descriptions and counted quantities, from the product list you upload and the counts you enter.
  • Your account: your name, username and password. Your manager creates the account; the password is stored by Google Firebase Authentication in encrypted form and nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Activity log: a record of who signed in or out, who counted which item and when, and changes to staff accounts. Only admins in your organisation can see it. It helps them check counts and spot mistakes.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or any other personal information. We don't use analytics or tracking, and we don't show ads.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are never saved or uploaded.

Where data is kept

Stock data is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore so that everyone counting sees the same numbers in real time. Each business's data is kept separately, and only that business's own accounts can read it. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Data is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Purchases

The full version is bought through the App Store or Google Play, and Apple or Google handle the payment; we never see card details. To check that a business has bought it we use RevenueCat, which receives the purchase record and an identifier for the business (not your name). RevenueCat processes this under its privacy policy.

Deleting data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: tap your name, then Delete my account. The person who created a business can delete the whole business the same way (Delete business): this removes its product list, counts, staff accounts and activity log. Your admin can also switch your account off. Starting a new stock check deletes the previous product list and counts. To have all data deleted, including the activity log, contact us at the address below.
