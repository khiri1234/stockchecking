The H Stock Management

The H Stock Management lets your team count stock together with their phones while you follow every count live on the dashboard. This page explains how to get started and answers common questions.

Getting started (managers)

  1. On a computer, open stock.hatef.cc/dashboard.html and create your admin account.
  2. Go to Stock check, upload your product list (Excel or CSV) and click Start new stock check with this list. Barcode and description columns are found automatically.
  3. Go to Staff and add a username and password for each person who will count.
  4. Optional: on Areas & tasks, split the shop into areas (for example Main Shop, Warehouse) and give each person their own list.

Counting (staff)

  1. Open the app on your phone, or go to stock.hatef.cc, and sign in with the username and password your manager gave you.
  2. Scan a barcode with the camera or a handheld scanner, type the quantity and tap Save.
  3. Found the same item somewhere else? Scan it again and add the new amount. The app adds the counts together.
  4. For many small items, switch on Quick count: every scan adds one.

Common questions

I forgot my password

Ask your manager. On the dashboard they open Staff and choose Reset password next to your name. To change a password you know, tap your name in the app and choose Change password.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Counts are saved on the phone and upload automatically when the connection comes back. Keep the app open until it shows that all counts are uploaded.

The camera doesn't scan

Make sure the app is allowed to use the camera (iPhone: Settings > The H Stock Management > Camera; Android: Settings > Apps > The H Stock Management > Permissions). Hold the barcode flat, in good light, about 15 cm from the camera. You can always type the barcode instead.

A count looks wrong

A manager can open Recounts on the dashboard and ask someone else to count the item again without seeing the first number, then choose which count to keep.

How do I get the results?

On the dashboard, open Stock check and choose Export results. You get an Excel file with every count, who made it, when and where.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone, Android phones, Windows and Mac computers, and any modern web browser at stock.hatef.cc.

How do I delete my account or my data?

In the app, tap your name and choose Delete my account. To have all of your organisation's data deleted, email us at the address above. See the privacy policy for details.
